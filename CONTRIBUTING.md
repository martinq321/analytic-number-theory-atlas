# Contributing

Use the site’s Contribute page or open an issue. Provide exact wording, assumptions, mathematical setting, original source and theorem/page locator. Clearly distinguish published results, conjectures and unpublished proposals. For an implication, list every joint premise and the conclusion.

Maintainers: acknowledge proposals; request a source check; record the reviewer, scope and date of review; accept through a pull request linked to the issue; credit the contributor. Never equate a citation check with a proof audit. Keep research proposals distinct from reference claims. Do not strengthen imported claims without evidence. Treat AI output as an unchecked draft.

For new topics, explain a concrete researcher use case and identify sources and prospective maintainers. Discuss scope before bulk imports. Do not upload third-party books or personal information.
